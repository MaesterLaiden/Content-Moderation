# Content Moderator (Volunteer, Remote)

**Duration:** 2024
**Type:** Volunteer Role (Remote)

---

## 📌 Overview

As a **Content Moderator (Volunteer)**, I was responsible for reviewing user-submitted content, ensuring compliance with safety and community standards, and escalating sensitive cases under **strict SLAs**. This role required strong **attention to detail, decision-making, and real-time monitoring**, which are directly transferable to IT Support and Security Operations (SOC) workflows.

---

## 🛠️ Responsibilities

* Reviewed flagged content (text, images, reports) for compliance with established guidelines.
* Monitored and triaged high-volume queues, applying **risk assessment models** to identify safety-critical cases.
* Escalated sensitive or policy-violating content to higher-level reviewers using structured workflows.
* Documented case details, escalation steps, and outcomes in a ticket-style format for audit purposes.
* Collaborated with other moderators remotely, ensuring **consistent enforcement of policies** across the platform.

---

## 📈 Achievements

* Reviewed **1,000+ content items** with 98% accuracy under SLA.
* Reduced false-positive escalations by improving decision-making criteria and documenting clearer escalation notes.
* Built transferable skills in **real-time monitoring, queue management, and escalation workflows** — directly applicable to Tier 1 IT Helpdesk and SOC Analyst roles.

---

## 🔑 Skills Gained

* **Attention to Detail**: Evaluated large volumes of content while maintaining high accuracy.
* **Escalation Processes**: Practiced structured escalation workflows similar to IT ticket handoffs (Tier 1 → Tier 2).
* **Risk Assessment**: Applied quick judgment under pressure for safety-sensitive cases.
* **Remote Collaboration**: Worked with a distributed team using digital communication tools.
* **Documentation**: Maintained clear and structured case records for compliance.

---

## 🎯 Relevance to IT / SOC Roles

While this role was focused on content safety, the workflows directly align with IT support and SOC monitoring:

* **Ticket queues = Content queues**
* **Escalation to higher reviewers = Tier 1 → Tier 2 escalation**
* **Content safety decisions = Incident triage decisions**

This experience sharpened my ability to handle **real-time monitoring and structured decision-making**, core skills needed in both **Helpdesk** and **Security Operations Center (SOC)** roles.
